Nikola Tesla

1856 - 1943

About Tesla

Nikola Tesla was born on 10 July 1856 in Smiljan and died on 7 January 1943 in New York. He studied engineering in Graz and moved to the United States in 1884. Tesla designed the alternating current (AC) motor and the AC power system that we still use today. He also invented the Tesla coil and worked on wireless energy.

Important events

  1. 1856 - born in Smiljan
  2. 1884 - moves to the United States
  3. 1888 - patents the AC induction motor
  4. 1891 - invents the Tesla coil
  5. 1943 - dies in New York

Main inventions

  • AC induction motor
  • AC power system
  • Tesla coil
  • Radio-controlled boat
